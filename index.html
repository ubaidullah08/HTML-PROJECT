OUR CULINARY PHILOSOPHY

Artisanal Street Craft Meets Modern Simplicity

At KTCH, we believe food should be uncomplicated, comforting, and prepared with high-grade, honest ingredients. From traditional Koshihikari rice musubi to double-smashed wagyu burgers and slow-fermented pizzas, every plate is crafted to order.

01

Fresh Daily Ingredients

We source directly from local California farms and specialty Japanese purveyors. Our sauces, dips, and crispy toppings are made from scratch each morning.

02

High-Heat Precision

Searing cast-iron flat tops, double-fried Japanese karaage, and roaring ovens give each dish its signature crunch, aroma, and delicate savoriness.

03

Fast, Intuitive Ordering

Whether you’re dining in at one of our 8 floor tables, picking up from our Bay Area food truck, or booking a table reservation, our platform keeps wait times brief and food piping hot.
